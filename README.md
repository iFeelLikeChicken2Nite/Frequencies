Frequencies is a stylish internet radio player that is simple on the surface, but built on a powerful framework which allows for a lot of room for customization and options. It sets out to provide a rich, unified, modern day feature set for making listening to the radio as user friendly as streaming on Spotify, but more flexible than most other radio streaming applications.

## Features that set Frequencies apart:

On top of your typical radio apps that allow you to stream from URLs and browse radio station directories, Frequencies allows for broader capabilities, such as...

* Ability to create custom stations from local files, YouTube stations, Twitch streams, and iBroadcast libraries
* Tags system for easily queuing your desired stations
* Built in Shazam
* Option to cover the forced ads you hear on some stations with your own music
* A regularly updated list of recommended radio stations that are hand picked by me, a human
* Per station equalizer options
* Volume equalization across stations
* Live, timed lyrics
* Optional, immersive static to bridge the silent gap between stations
* Song logging based on Shazam recognition
* A built in, experimental web browser designed to let you easily "steal" an embedded audio player's stream URL from a list 
* Ability to change station's displayed name and associated artwork
* Automatic skipping of stations that are down
* Easy, modern sharing options
* Winamp skin support
* Artist biographies
* Built in Project M visualizers
* Ability to sync between devices on your network
* Android Auto and Pebble support
* Compatibility with over 10 popular radio apps

Back in the day, if you wanted to take a drive, you'd usually just let the radio play, and it meant you were exposed to things you sometimes weren't sure you liked. It challenged your taste and helped you shape your own opinions. Now, Spotify tells you what you are going to like, whether you "like" it or not!

<img width="984" height="1035" alt="Screenshot_20261018_183143" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/02106067-a500-4c42-86b0-2f446517d07e" />

Frequencies is intended to replicate the nostalgia and experience of an FM radio, if your radio were on crack. You can replace your lousy, low quality country twang filled FM radio stations with entirely hand selected stations, and enhance the ones available to you locally that are worth listening to with higher quality digital streaming.

This lets you just get going, and enjoy what you're hearing without burdening you with the tough decision of what you want to put on. Every time you hit skip could be a meaningful decision that leads you down a musical rabbit hole, instead of an exhausting chore of chasing the more boring parts of your music library in endless circles.
